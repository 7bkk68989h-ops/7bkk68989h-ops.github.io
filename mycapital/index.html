MyCapital

All your investments in one place.

MyCapital is a free app for iPhone and Android to track your investments: stocks, ETFs, crypto, P2P lending, cash and more. See the total value of your portfolios, your gains and losses, dividends and how your money grows over the years.

What you can do

  • Organise your investments in portfolios and accounts.
  • Follow prices, charts and exchange rates from public market data.
  • Record buys, sells, dividends, interest, deposits and withdrawals.
  • See yearly charts of what you deposited and what you earned.
  • Import and export your transactions as CSV.

Your data stays with you

There are no accounts and no servers of ours. Everything you enter stays on your device and, if you choose, in your own cloud backup. The app is free and shows ads, which you can remove with a one-time purchase.
